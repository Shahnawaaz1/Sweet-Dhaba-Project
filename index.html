Recipes & Ingredients (sample)

Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Milk Powder
  • Sugar
  • Ghee
  • Cardamom

Recipe

  1. Mix milk powder & ghee to form dough.
  2. Make small balls and deep fry till golden.
  3. Soak in warm sugar syrup flavoured with cardamom.

Barfi Sweet

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Bread
  • oil

Recipe

  1. Barfi, sweet and creamy, melts in your mouth delight.
  2. Make small balls and deep fry till golden.
  3. Soak in warm sugar syrup flavoured with cardamom.
